## Diversification

Diversification combines investments whose returns do not move exactly together. When correlation is below +1, the assets' movements partially offset one another, so the combined portfolio can have less volatility than either asset alone.

For the 40% IG-bond / 60% equity benchmark, the variance is:

`(0.40^2 × 0.06^2) + (0.60^2 × 0.16^2) + (2 × 0.40 × 0.60 × 0.25 × 0.06 × 0.16) = 0.010944`

The square root is `0.1046`, or 10.46%. The correlation term is essential; leaving it out incorrectly produces 9.90%.
