# COST — Costco Wholesale Corporation

## Buffett-style business memo

**Verdict: Own the business.**

Costco's five-year record shows a business that has expanded while continuing
to generate cash. Revenue grew from **$226,954 million** at the fiscal year
ended **2022-08-28** to **$303,154 million** at **2026-08-30**. Over that same
period, net income increased from **$5,844 million** to **$9,226 million**.

### The owner-earnings test

Owner earnings were positive in every reported year and rose from
**$3,501 million** to **$9,382 million**. The series was not perfectly smooth
— owner earnings were **$6,745 million** in 2023-09-03 and **$6,629 million**
in 2024-09-01 — but the first-to-last direction is clearly higher.

### The return-on-equity test

ROE exceeded the 15% threshold in every year: **28.3%**, **25.1%**, **31.2%**,
**27.8%**, and **25.8%**. That consistency, combined with positive and growing
owner earnings, supports the view that Costco has been a durable,
cash-generative business.

| Buffett check | Result |
|---|---|
| ROE of 15% or more every year | Pass |
| Owner earnings positive every year | Pass |
| Owner earnings grew, first year to last | Pass |

This is a judgment of business quality only. It does **not** judge Costco's
market price, valuation, or whether the stock is a buy today.

**Decision:** I trust this verdict because every conclusion above comes from
the SEC EDGAR values and computed checks printed by the script.
